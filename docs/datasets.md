# Datasets

The datasets are not redistributed here. They are publicly available from their
original sources.

## Thin smear (training) and external thin-smear evaluation
- NIH / NLM Malaria Cell Images dataset (27,558 segmented single-cell images:
  13,779 parasitized, 13,779 uninfected). Rajaraman et al., 2018.
  NIH malaria datasets repository: https://ceb.nlm.nih.gov/repositories/malaria-datasets/
  Related NIH resource page: https://lhncbc.nlm.nih.gov/LHC-research/LHC-projects/image-processing/malaria-datasheet.html

## Thick smear (training) and independent thin-smear external evaluation
- Nelson Mandela African Institution of Science and Technology (NM-AIST) Malaria
  Dataset, collected in Tanzania. Lufyagila et al., 2024.
  Harvard Dataverse DOI: https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/O2WVWA

Set the dataset paths at the top of each notebook to match your local or Kaggle setup.
